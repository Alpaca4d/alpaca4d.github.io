## The numbers, if someone asks

- **What it gives:** a diagram along the cut of the moment, the shear or the membrane force, and
  the totals N, Vy, Vz, Mx, My and Mz through it, the way a beam's section carries them.
- **What it is for:** the moment across a design strip of a slab, the forces at the base of a shear
  wall or a core, the shear along a support line.
- **Its menu:** the component and unit of the diagram, smooth across shells, a diagram scale, the
  section's axes and the values written on it.
