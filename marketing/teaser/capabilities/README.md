# Alpaca4d teaser: LinkedIn post

A 44-second teaser of everything Alpaca4d does, made from the example videos in `marketing/`: the
title, ten chapters (24 shots from 40 clips) and the end card, cut to a 120 BPM beat. Made by
`Tools/Marketing/Social/teaser.py` in the Alpaca4d repository; run it again when the clips change. Here: the
video (`alpaca4d-teaser.mp4`) and a still from it (`alpaca4d-teaser-poster.png`).

**Wait before posting:** the video shows Pushover Analysis, J2 Plasticity and Footfall Analysis, which
are not in a Yak beta yet. Post it once they are, or cut those chapters out of `teaser.py` first.

## What to attach

- **The post:** `alpaca4d-teaser.mp4`, 1920 x 1080, 44 s. It has no sound; each chapter is named on
  screen. A 120 BPM track laid under it lands on the cuts.

## The numbers, if someone asks

What each chapter shows, in order:

| Chapter | The examples |
| --- | --- |
| Static analysis | truss roof hall, steel tower, vertical truss facade, barrel vault, compressed shell |
| Natural vibration | concrete tank (a hundred modes), steel tower (its first mode), the Rebellious Slave (two modes) |
| Earthquakes | the city and the cable-stayed bridge (in real time), four towers of 100 storeys and more and the twisting building, the response spectrum sticks |
| Pushover and plasticity | the irregular concrete building, the beam with holes, the pipe T-joint, yield lines, the plate with a hole, the thick pipe |
| Footfall | the double-landing stair, the helical stair |
| 3D concrete printing | the cylinder, the twisted column, the leaning column, the dome: printed, then buckling |
| Section optimiser | the curved beam with a moving load, the beam grid with moving supports |
| Design explorer | the Explorer's sample study of 108 footbridges: filtered on utilisation, deflection and frequency, sorted by mass, the lightest opened in 3D |
| Topology optimisation | the bridge (its whole run), the multi-material truss node |
| Principal stress lines | the deep beam with a load walking along its top, the Nervi umbrellas |

The earthquakes are El Centro (1940). The city and the cable-stayed bridge play in real time; the towers
and the spectrum three times as fast, the bridge's optimisation about three times, the other
optimisations and the prints four to eight times. The Explorer's sample study has
results estimated by hand, so the post quotes none of them.
