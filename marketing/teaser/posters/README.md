# Alpaca4d posters: LinkedIn carousel

Twelve posters in the teaser's look, 4:5 (2160 x 2700), for one LinkedIn post: a cover, a poster
for each of the ten things the teaser shows, and an end card. Made by `Tools/Marketing/Social/posters.py`
in the Alpaca4d repository from the stills in `marketing/`; run it again when they change. Here: the
twelve PNGs, and the same twelve as one PDF (alpaca4d-carousel.pdf) for a document post.

**Wait before posting:** posters 04 (pushover) and 05 (footfall) show Pushover Analysis and Footfall
Analysis, which are not in a Yak beta yet. Post once they are, or leave those two out.

## What to attach

- **The post:** the PDF (alpaca4d-carousel.pdf) as a document: on LinkedIn, *Add a document*, choose
  it and give it the title "Everything Alpaca4d can do"; people swipe through it page by page. Its
  first page is `00-cover.png`, the nine-picture mosaic, which also works alone.
- **A carousel instead:** the twelve PNGs in order as pictures (LinkedIn shows the first four in a
  grid and the rest behind a "+8"): the cover, then `01-frames-trusses-shells.png`, `02-every-mode.png`, `03-earthquakes.png`, `04-past-the-elastic-limit.png`, `05-feel-every-footstep.png`, `06-print-it-before-it-buckles.png`, `07-every-member-sized.png`, `08-explore-every-option.png`, `09-only-the-material-it-needs.png`, `10-see-the-forces-flow.png`, `11-try-it-free.png`.
- **Alt text:** A poster titled "Everything Alpaca4d can do." with nine pictures of structural
  analyses: a hundred tank modes, a tower in an earthquake, concrete umbrellas with stress lines, a
  truss node in two materials, a helical stair, a twisted printed column, a vault, a building with
  plastic hinges and a grid of sized beams.
