## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The bridge:** a Warren truss 12 m long, six panels of 2 m, 2 m deep; steel tubes 100 mm across
  with a 5 mm wall, S355; 23 bars on 13 joints.
- **The alpaca:** 0.6 kN on the middle joint of the deck.
- **The forces:** top chord −0.90 kN, bottom chord 0.75 kN, diagonals ±0.335 kN, by hand and in
  OpenSees.
- **Try:** 1 m deep, the top chord carries 1.8 kN; tension-only bars go slack and the analysis
  fails.
