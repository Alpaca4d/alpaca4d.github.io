## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The deck:** C24 timber, 1.2 m wide and 120 mm deep, 10 m span on a pin and a roller.
- **The herd:** an alpaca of 60 kg on every metre; with the deck's own 59 kg, 119 kg a metre.
- **The first mode:** 1.94 Hz by hand and in OpenSees; people walk at about two steps a second.
- **Try:** twice as deep, 4.48 Hz; a tonne of sandbags, 1.2 Hz; no herd, 2.75 Hz; a pier in the
  middle, 7.75 Hz (7.76 by hand).
- **The comfort limit:** Eurocode 0 asks for a comfort check below 5 Hz.
