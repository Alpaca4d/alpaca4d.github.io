# Playground, the island: LinkedIn post

The Playground's island keeps your progress: finish a lesson and its structure is built in colour
on the island, the next area opens and the alpaca walks on to it. Here: a screen recording of the
River's last round, the lesson done, and the way back to the island (`island-progress.mp4`, and
`island-progress.gif`), the island at the start (`01-island-start.png`), after the River
(`02-after-the-river.png`), with all ten areas done (`03-all-done.png`), and on a phone
(`04-phone.png`). All recorded in Chrome, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844.

## What to attach

- **The post:** `island-progress.mp4`, 16 seconds: the answer, 24.4 mm, then back to the island,
  where the plank turns orange and the alpaca walks to the cliff.

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The island:** ten areas, from a plank over a river to a bridge across a chasm, each a lesson
  that builds a real Grasshopper definition.
- **The plank:** C24 timber, 300 × 50 mm, 4 m span; the alpaca, 0.6 kN, in the middle: 24.4 mm by
  hand (PL³/48EI) and in OpenSees.
- **Progress** stays in the browser: no account. A lesson can be played again, and a locked area
  played straight away.
