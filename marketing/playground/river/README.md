# Playground, the River: LinkedIn post

plank across a 4 m river, with the alpaca standing in the middle. Here: a screenshot of each step
(`01-line.png` to `07-run.png`), the lesson done with its definition (`08-done.png`), the game on a
phone (`09-phone.png`), a wrong pick that stands the plank on its edge (`10-on-its-edge-frames.png`)
and the plank close up (`11-the-plank.png`). `04-supports.png` shows a wrong pick and what it
would do.

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The plank:** C24 timber, 300 mm wide and 50 mm thick, lying flat, 4 m span, a pin and a roller.
- **The alpaca:** 0.6 kN, about 60 kg, in the middle.
- **How far it bends:** 24.4 mm by hand (PL³/48EI) and in OpenSees; the beam's shear adds a
  hundredth of a millimetre.
- **Try:** on its edge, 0.7 mm, 36 times less; built in at both ends, 6.1 mm, a quarter.
- **The plank breaks** at about 3 kN in the middle.
