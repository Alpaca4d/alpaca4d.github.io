# Playground, the Market: LinkedIn post

Lesson 6 of the Playground, the website's island game that teaches Alpaca4d in ten lessons: a market
stall's shelf on two trestles, a crate in the span and the alpaca on the overhang. Nothing is built
here: the player predicts the reactions and the moments, then reads them from the model. Here:
wrong predictions (`01-tips-off-trestle-a.png`, `03-half-each-tips.png`), the stall
(`02-the-stall.png`), the right prediction (`04-predicted.png`), the readings (`05-reactions.png`,
`06-shear.png`, `07-bending.png`), the lesson done (`08-done.png`), the shelf close up
(`09-the-shelf.png`) and the game on a phone (`10-phone.png`).

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The shelf:** C24 timber, 300 × 50 mm, flat, 4 m long; trestle A a pin at its start, trestle B a
  roller 3 m along; eight elements of 0.5 m.
- **The loads:** a 1 kN crate 1.5 m along, the 0.6 kN alpaca at 4 m.
- **The reactions:** 0.3 kN at trestle A, 1.3 kN at trestle B.
- **The moments:** +0.45 kNm under the crate, −0.6 kNm over trestle B; the shear +0.3, −0.7, +0.6 kN.
- **Try:** trestle B at 2 m, trestle A reads −0.35 kN; trestle B at the end, 0.94 kNm under the crate.
