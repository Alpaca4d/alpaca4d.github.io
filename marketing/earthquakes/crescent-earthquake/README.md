## The numbers, if someone asks

- **Roof tips:** 168 mm at the most, 6.2 s in; the middle of the outer face 3% less
- **Periods:** 1.6, 1.6 and 1.5 s
- **The block:** 12 bays along an arc 30 m from its middle, 2 bays of 7.5 m deep, concrete columns 0.7 x 0.7 m, beams 0.4 x 0.7 m, 220 mm slabs
- **The record:** El Centro 1940, north-south, 0.32 g at its peak, 2 s in; linear elastic, Newmark, Rayleigh damping, 5%.
