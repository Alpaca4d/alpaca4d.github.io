## The numbers, if someone asks

- **Roof:** 236 mm at the most, 6.4 s in
- **Periods:** 2.2 s along the bow, 2.0 s across (1.9 s straight)
- **The tower:** 40 storeys of 3.6 m, three rings of 16 steel box columns 700 x 40 mm, IPE600 beams, 200 mm slabs
- **The record:** El Centro 1940, north-south, 0.32 g at its peak, 2 s in; linear elastic, Newmark, Rayleigh damping, 5%.
