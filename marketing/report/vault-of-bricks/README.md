# Vault of bricks report: an example to share

An Alpaca4d example report of bricks: a concrete barrel vault 8 m across, 2.5 m high, 6 m long and 400 mm thick, all solids: 1,440 SSP bricks, five layers through its thickness, under its own weight.

It was written by the Report component from the definition `Tools/Examples/Report/ReportBricks.gh` in the Alpaca4d repository - the structure its own example builds, with the analyses and the report beside it. Every picture was drawn by the report itself, there is no Model View on the canvas for it, and the headings are numbered by the report.

HOLD: the Report component is only on the `report-module` branch, in no Yak beta, and has no example page on the website. Share once it is released.

## Files

- `vault-of-bricks-report.pdf`: the report, 6 pages, 5 tables and 10 pictures.
- `vault-of-bricks-page-1.png` to `vault-of-bricks-page-6.png`: each page as a picture.
- `vault-of-bricks-pages.png`: all the pages on one sheet.
- `grasshopper-definition.png`: the definition on the canvas.

## What is in it

- The model: its summary, and a picture with its supports.
- Static analysis: the displacement, the Von Mises stress, and the reactions, with their largest and smallest values written on.
- Natural vibration: the first six modes, with their frequencies and participating mass.

## Alt text

Pages of an analysis report of vault of bricks: tables of the model and its results, and pictures of its displacement, forces and modes in colour, each with its largest and smallest value written on it.
