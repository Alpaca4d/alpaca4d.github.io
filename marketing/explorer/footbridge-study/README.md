# The Explorer on a footbridge study: LinkedIn post

The Alpaca4d Explorer, the web page that opens a design study made with the Explorer components in
Grasshopper, used on its sample: 108 steel truss footbridges over their span, depth, panels and
chords. In the video, three axes are filtered one after the other (utilisation up to 0.9, deflection up
to 80 mm, frequency from 3 Hz), 59 designs are left, the gallery is sorted by mass and the lightest is
opened and turned round in 3D. Made by `Tools/Marketing/Social/explorer.py`, which drives the live page in a
headless Chrome. Here: the video (`explorer-study.mp4`) and a still from it (`explorer-study-poster.png`).

## What to attach

- **The post:** `explorer-study.mp4`, 1920 x 1080, 8 s, no sound.
- **A carousel instead:** none; the still `explorer-study-poster.png` alone, or with
  `marketing/examples/footbridge-study.png`.
- **Alt text:** The Alpaca4d Explorer in a browser: 108 footbridge designs drawn as coloured lines on
  parallel axes. Three axes are filtered, the lines that fail turn grey, the gallery of pictures
  shrinks, and the lightest bridge that passes opens in a 3D view and turns round.

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The study:** 108 designs: spans 20 to 36 m, depths 1.4 to 3 m, 6 to 10 panels, HEA160, 200 or 240
  chords. The sample's results were estimated by hand to show the page, not analysed: quote none of
  them.
- **The filters in the video:** utilisation up to 0.9, deflection up to 80 mm (span / 250 at 20 m),
  first frequency from 3 Hz; 59 of the 108 designs pass.
