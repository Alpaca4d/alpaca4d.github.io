# Footbridge Study: LinkedIn post

A steel truss footbridge and a study of 108 designs over its span, depth, panels and chords, opened
in the Alpaca4d Explorer. One picture in this folder: `footbridge-study.png`, the Explorer on the
sample study, with the parallel axes, the gallery and one design.

## What to attach

- **The post:** `footbridge-study.png`. It is the Explorer on its sample study, whose results were estimated by hand; the post quotes none of them.
- **A carousel instead:** none; this folder has the one picture for it.
- **Alt text:** The Alpaca4d Explorer open on the footbridge study: 108 designs on parallel axes, a
  gallery, and one design with its picture, inputs and results.

## The numbers, if someone asks

| Input | Values |
| --- | --- |
| Span | 20, 25, 30, 35 m |
| Depth | 1.5, 2.25, 3 m |
| Panels | 6, 8, 10 |
| Chord | HEA 160, HEA 200, HEA 240 |

- **The designs:** 4 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 108.
- **Saved with each:** mass, largest deflection, highest utilisation, first natural frequency, a
  picture and the 3D model.
- **The picture's numbers:** from the sample study, estimated by hand, not analysed. Do not quote
  them as Alpaca4d results.
- **Licence, if asked:** Explorer Inputs and Explorer Record need a licence; without one, Run Analysis takes models of up to 50 elements and the Results components read them as numbers (https://alpaca4d.github.io/docs/references/license.html).
