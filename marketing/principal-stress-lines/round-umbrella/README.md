A round concrete slab 16 m across built into one column, after Nervi's round umbrella, under a uniform
load, and its bending lines from Principal Stress Lines; for the pictures, the lines extruded down into
ribs. Here: the lines growing from above (`round-umbrella-growing.mp4`), walking under it

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The slab:** concrete, a disc 16 m across, 200 mm thick, built into one column over a head 1 m
  across, under 5 kN/m².
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines in bending, 0.55 m apart, from a seed turned round the middle
  four times.
- **The ribs** in the other pictures are only for the pictures, 350 mm deep; the analysis is the flat
  slab.
- **Licence:** the slab is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for these
  pictures.
