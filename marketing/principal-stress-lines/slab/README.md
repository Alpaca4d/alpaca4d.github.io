## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The slab:** concrete, 12 x 8 m, 250 mm thick, 30 x 20 shells, pinned along every edge.
- **The video:** the uniform load off, 300 kN on one node of the middle line, moved along it by a slider;
  solved again at every position.
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines in bending. They stop where the two principal moments are equal,
  as in the middle of a simply supported edge, because there the field gives no direction.
- **Licence:** 600 shells is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for these
  pictures.
