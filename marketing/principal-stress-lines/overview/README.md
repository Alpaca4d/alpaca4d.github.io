# Four Plates: LinkedIn post

Four concrete plates and their principal stress lines, in one definition: a cantilever wall and a deep
beam in their own plane, a slab on four edges in bending, and a curved barrel canopy cantilevering from
its root. Here: the four together (`all-four.png`) and the canvas (`grasshopper-definition.png`). There is
no video; each plate has a folder of its own.

## What to attach

- **The post:** `all-four.png`, the four plates with their supports and loads.
- **A carousel instead:** `all-four.png`, `grasshopper-definition.png`.
- **Alt text:** Four concrete plates side by side - a wall held at one end, a deep beam on two supports,
  a slab on four edges and a curved canopy - each covered in purple and blue lines.

## The numbers, if someone asks

| Plate | Size | Held | Load | Lines |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cantilever wall | 8 x 2.5 m, 250 mm | built in along one end | 50 kN/m along its top | in its plane |
| Deep beam | 10 x 4 m, 300 mm | the bottom 0.4 m of each end | 500 kN on its top | in its plane |
| Slab | 12 x 8 m, 250 mm | all four edges | 10 kN/m² | bending |
| Canopy | 7 m out, 6 m wide, 1.5 m rise, 100 mm | built in along its root | its own weight | in its surface |

- **One switch:** Principal Stress Lines draws the membrane lines or the bending lines; they are generally
  not the same field.
