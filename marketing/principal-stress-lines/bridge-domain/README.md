deck, analysed solid as it is, with its principal stress lines through its middle plane. Here: the block
from the front with its lines (`bridge-domain.png`), the canvas (`grasshopper-definition.png`) and the
canvas beside the result (`bridge-domain-in-grasshopper.png`). There is no video.

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The block:** steel, 16 m long, 2 m wide and 4 m high, SSP bricks, pinned along both ends of its
  bottom; its top layer a deck carrying 10 kN/m². It is the design domain of the Topology Bridge
  example, analysed whole, before any optimisation.
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines on Solids, a field grown from seeds in the block's middle plane.
- **Licence:** the block is over the free 50 elements.
