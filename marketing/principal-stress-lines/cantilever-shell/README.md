# Cantilever Shell: LinkedIn post

A curved concrete canopy, a barrel 7 m out from its root and 6 m wide, built in along its root under its
own weight, and its principal stress lines in its surface. Here: the canopy spinning
(`cantilever-shell-spin.mp4`) and walked under (`cantilever-shell-walk-through.mp4`), each with a `.gif`;
the canopy with and without its support (`cantilever-shell.png`, `cantilever-shell-clean.png`), from its
tip (`cantilever-shell-tip.png`), the canvas (`grasshopper-definition.png`) and the canvas beside the
result (`cantilever-shell-in-grasshopper.png`).

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The canopy:** a barrel 7 m out from its root and 6 m wide, rising 1.5 m across its width on a
  parabola; concrete, 100 mm thick, 35 x 30 shells.
- **Held:** built in along its root; under its own weight.
- **The lines:** membrane principal stress lines, from one seed in the middle of the canopy.
- **Licence:** 1 050 shells is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for
  these pictures.
