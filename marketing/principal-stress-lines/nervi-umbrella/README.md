## The numbers, if someone asks

- **One umbrella:** concrete, 15 x 15 m, 200 mm thick, 60 x 60 shells, built into its column over a head
  1 m square, under 5 kN/m².
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines in bending, from one seed off the diagonals.
- **For the pictures:** the lines smoothed and extruded 350 mm down into ribs, the column a pipe 8 m down
  to a floor, and nine umbrellas 1 m apart, three by three. Each is analysed alone, as Nervi's stand
  apart.
- **Licence:** 3 600 shells is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for
  these pictures.
