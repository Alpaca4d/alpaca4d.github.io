## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The form:** a round net 30 m across, pinned on sixteen footings round its rim, pushed up by
  Kangaroo, its edges keeping their length.
- **The shell:** concrete, 120 mm thick, under its own weight; membrane lines, 0.6 m apart.
- **The seed:** on a symmetric dome both principal stresses are the same at the crown, so the lines start
  from a seed off the middle and off the axes, turned round the middle sixteen times.
- **Needs:** Kangaroo2, which Rhino 7 and 8 ship; a licence, as the shell is over the free 50 elements.
  No example page: the definition was made for these pictures.
