## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The slab:** concrete, 25 x 25 m, 200 mm thick, 100 x 100 shells; on 5 x 5 columns 5 m apart, pinned,
  reaching half a bay past the outer ones; under 10 kN/m².
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines in bending, from one seed inside a bay, off its middle, where the
  two principal moments are the same; 0.8 m apart.
- **The ribs:** only for the pictures, each line smoothed and extruded 300 mm down; the columns are pipes
  4 m down to a floor. The analysis is the flat slab.
- **Licence:** 10 000 shells is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for
  these pictures.
