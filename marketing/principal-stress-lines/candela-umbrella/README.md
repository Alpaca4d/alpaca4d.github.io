## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The shape:** 12 x 12 m in plan, four hyperbolic paraboloids, its height a formula of x and y. No
  form-finding.
- **The shell:** concrete, 80 mm thick, built into one column over a head 1 m square, under its own
  weight; membrane lines, 0.35 m apart.
- **The hall:** nine copies 12.5 m apart, only for the pictures; the analysis is one umbrella alone.
- **Licence:** the shell is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for these
  pictures.
