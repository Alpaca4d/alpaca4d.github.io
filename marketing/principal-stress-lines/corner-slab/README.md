## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The slab:** concrete, 12 x 12 m, 250 mm thick, 48 x 48 shells, pinned on four corner columns, under
  10 kN/m².
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines in bending, 0.45 m apart, from a seed off the middle and off its
  axes, turned round four times: at the middle the two principal moments are the same.
- **The ribs** in the other pictures are only for the pictures; the analysis is the flat slab.
- **Licence:** 2 304 shells is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for
  these pictures.
