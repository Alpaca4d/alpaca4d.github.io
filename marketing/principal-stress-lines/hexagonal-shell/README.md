# Hexagonal Shell: LinkedIn post

A hexagonal shell 24 m across on its six corners, form-found by Kangaroo and analysed with Alpaca4d under
its own weight, with its principal stress lines in its surface. Here: walking under it
(`hexagonal-shell-walk-through.mp4`), the form being found (`hexagonal-shell-form-finding.mp4`), the
lines growing (`hexagonal-shell-growing.mp4`), the shell spinning (`hexagonal-shell-spin.mp4`), each
with a `.gif`; from above, below and outside (`hexagonal-shell.png`, `-clean.png`, `-top.png`,
`-top-clean.png`, `-below.png`, `-outside.png`), the canvas (`grasshopper-definition.png`) and the canvas
beside the result (`hexagonal-shell-in-grasshopper.png`).

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The form:** a triangulated hexagonal net 24 m across, pinned at its six corners, pushed up by
  Kangaroo, its edges keeping their length.
- **The shell:** concrete, 100 mm thick, under its own weight; membrane lines, 0.5 m apart.
- **Needs:** Kangaroo2, which Rhino 7 and 8 ship; a licence, as the shell is over the free 50 elements.
