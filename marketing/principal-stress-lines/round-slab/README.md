## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The slab:** concrete, a disc 20 m across, 250 mm thick, pinned on eight columns in a ring 14 m
  across, under 10 kN/m².
- **The lines:** Principal Stress Lines in bending, 0.5 m apart, from a seed turned round the middle
  eight times.
- **The ribs:** only for the pictures; the analysis is the flat slab.
- **Licence:** the slab is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for these
  pictures.
