own weight, with the principal stress lines in their surface. Here: walking under it

## The numbers, if someone asks

- **The shape:** 12 x 12 m in plan, rising 4 m; at every point the higher of two barrels, so it has a
  crease along each diagonal. No form-finding.
- **The shell:** concrete, 150 mm thick, pinned at its four corners, under its own weight; membrane
  lines, 0.35 m apart.
- **Licence:** the shell is over the free 50 elements. No example page: the definition was made for these
  pictures.
