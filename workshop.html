Tutor: Marco Pellegrino

The day workshop is intended for beginner to intermediate users of Alpaca4d. Marco will explore with you more some techniques of working with complex parametric structural models through scripting using Grasshopper.


Note The workshop has been set up in order to self sponsor the development and it will allow to get resources needed to keep up to speed the implementations. 😊

Day 1 – Get Started with Alpaca4D
  • Introduction
    •
  • Definition of a structural model with beams, shells, supports and loads.
  • Results retrieval and display for beams, shells and supports.

Course requirements: Prior knowledge of Grasshopper is strongly recommended. OpenSees is only recommended but not mandatory. Rhino 7 licenses can be downloaded and use with 90 days trial version.

Online course fees: EUR 395,- (+VAT); full-time students get a 50% discount (proof of status required). Please note, your seat is confirmed only after payment has cleared.

Min. number of participants for each course: 4. If there is no quorum, the course will be cancelled 15 days before.

Course language: English
