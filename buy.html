Terms and conditions.

What a license covers, and what it does not, OpenSees included, is set out on the License page of the docs.

  • A license is for the Alpaca4d plug-in only. OpenSees is not part of the purchase: it is software from UC Berkeley, which you download from them, subject to its own license. Buying Alpaca4d gives you no rights in OpenSees and takes none away.
  • A license is for one computer. It runs for 1 year from the date of supply, or from the end of your previous license if you renew before it ends.
  • When a license ends, Alpaca4d goes back to the free limits until you renew. Your Grasshopper files and models are not affected.
  • A Student license is for students, for study and research only, and not for commercial work. Send a valid student ID with your request. We use it only to check that you study, and delete it once checked.
  • A Professional license is for any work, commercial work included.
  • Support by email to alpaca4d@gmail.com while your license runs. We promise a maximum 48 hour response time unless prevented by deliverability problems. Notify us if you use a different email address from the one your product key is sent to.
  • We will email you a product key within 48 hours of receipt of payment. To renew, you may need to give your current product key. Get in touch if you change computer.
